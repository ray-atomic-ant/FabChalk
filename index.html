    01 / Services

    What we do

    01

    Coordination

    Electrical modeled and coordinated against structure, mechanical, plumbing and fire protection, so conflicts get settled in the model instead of at the ceiling. Raceway, cable tray, busway, gear and supports.

    You get: a coordinated model and clash log, signed off before install pricing.

    02

    Install drawings

    Drawings built for the foreman, not the file server: dimensions, elevations, hanger and support locations, and the details the crew actually asks about.

    You get: field-ready sheets, issued ahead of your crew and material.

    03

    Prefabrication

    Spool sheets, assembly drawings, cut lists and bills of material your shop can build from, sequenced to how the job will be installed.

    You get: shop-ready packages, in your prefab team’s format.

    04

    Constructability review

    We read the job the way an electrician would. Access, sequence, labor and material, flagged early, with options and what each one costs the field.

    You get: a short list of the calls that matter, before you price it.

    05

    Standards and content

    Templates, families, parameters and naming. We build to your standard, or help you write one your whole department can keep.

    You get: content and standards that outlast the job.

    06

    Automation and data

    We build our own tools for the repetitive work: quantities, tagging, sheet setup, model checks. The routine gets automated, so people spend their time on judgement.

    You get: faster turnaround on the parts no one should do by hand.

    02 / Engagement

    Two ways to work with us.

    For BIM managers and directors

    An extension of your team.

    Your standards, your templates, your naming, your tools. Your engineers set the direction, and we add capacity they can count on, job after job.

    • We work inside your environment, not beside it.
    • Your BIM engineers stay in charge of the model. We take the load off them.
    • We raise the flag early and hand it back clean.

    For project managers

    Scoped delivery.

    Hand us a scope and a schedule. We come back with what it takes, what it costs and when each milestone lands. How we get there is on us.

    • A price with every assumption written down.
    • Milestones tied to your schedule.
    • One person accountable for the whole scope.

    ●  About

    We exist to help the people who build our world, build it better.

    FabChalk started as one person showing small, specific pieces of electrical BIM. Today it is a team that does all of it, and is building toward much more.

    Founder film▶

    01 / Origin

    It started with a printer.

    Arjun’s five-year-old daughter typed her first sentence on his keyboard. They counted down together, five, four, three, two, one, and the printer started humming. She grabbed the page and ran to show her grandmother.

    That look, I made this and it works, is what we want every crew to have when the drawing matches the job.

    The name comes from the same place. Fab, for bringing something good to the drawing board. Chalk, for the chalk line on the slab, where the drawing meets the field.

    02 / Point of view

    Our point of view

    These aren’t taglines. They decide who we hire, how we train, what we charge for and which jobs we take.

    01

    Construction comes first.

    Technology serves the build, not the other way around. The field should never have to bend itself around the limits of our tools. If the process underneath is broken, more software won’t fix it.

    02

    Technical leadership makes better projects.

    Better projects come from better planning and better decisions, not more hours or a larger team. Our value is the judgement we bring, not the hours we log.

    03

    Learning compounds.

    Every job should leave the company, our clients and our people better prepared for the next one. We teach what we learn, inside and outside these walls.

    03 / Approach

    There’s no master key.

    Your job isn’t the same as anyone else’s. So we don’t show up with a key and look for a lock it fits. We find your lock first, then make the key with you.

    04 / Values

    How we work

    01

    Think like an owner.

    Does it reduce labor? Does it reduce risk? Does it make the field’s job easier? Would we pay for it ourselves?

    02

    03

    The company and the client should both be smarter after every job.

    Not through claims. Through work that holds up in the field.

    06

    Vision

    To elevate electrical construction by building the world’s most respected team of technical leaders.

    Mission

    We work with electrical contractors to solve complex construction problems through deep technical knowledge, digital delivery, constructability, prefabrication, automation and continuous learning. We measure success by the confidence we build in our clients, the growth of our teammates and the difference our work makes in the field.

    05 / A note from Arjun

    I’ve been in this mess too.

    We have capable people doing complicated work, and too much of it still runs on tribal knowledge, disconnected information and software that wasn’t built around how people actually build.

    A pretty model isn’t the same as a useful model. More tools don’t make a company more efficient. And “that’s just how construction works” isn’t a good enough answer for me.

    I’m building FabChalk to be a group of people who know construction deeply and use technology to fix real problems. People who make someone’s Tuesday afternoon easier, not just a presentation look good. I want to do work my daughter will be proud of when she grows up.

    If I’m wrong about something here, tell me. I’ll change my mind.

    Arjun, founder

    01 / Attitude

    What we look for

    We’ll take the right attitude over a long list of skills. Skills can be taught. These can’t.

    01

    Curiosity

    You want to know how a data center, a hospital or an airport gets powered because it’s interesting, not because it looks good on a résumé. You ask why we do it this way, and what happens if we don’t.

    02

    Ownership

    You see a problem, pick it up and own it. Then you help the person next to you own theirs. We want people who build leaders, starting with themselves.

    03

    Paving your own path

    You look at where we are today and see what we could be doing. Then you build it, and grow yourself and the company together.

    02 / Straight answers

    The questions you’re probably asking.

    Straight answers. Where something doesn’t exist yet, we say so.

    Will I have more influence here?

    Yes. There are very few layers between a good idea and how we work.

    Will I learn something new?

    On every job. And you’ll be expected to teach what you learn.

    Will I build something that lasts?

    Standards, tools and people that outlive the job they started on.

    Are the founders people I can trust?

    Watch the founder video, then ask us anything in the interview. We’d rather you decide with your eyes open.

    Is this company solving interesting problems?

    Data centers, hospitals, airports and industrial jobs, for electrical contractors who want more than a model. See the work

    The one question we ask about every hire

    Will this person let us solve a problem for clients that we can’t solve today?

    Six months or six years

    However long you stay, you should leave sharper than you came. We’d rather say that out loud. What we ask is that while you’re here, you make it better for everyone around you, whatever their skill level.
